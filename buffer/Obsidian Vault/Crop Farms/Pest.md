[[Farming]]

Appears inside farm fields, when multiple [[Farmland]] blocks are positioned in close proximity. 

The presence of pests is indicated by crops getting grey/desaturated, flying bug particles, and [[Rabbit]]s maybe playing a new animation when they try to eat the crop.

Infested crops nearby also increase the chance(range of time taken) of nearby crops being infected.
Infested crops stop growing
