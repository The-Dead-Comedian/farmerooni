


The Seagull is a passive mob. Their main purpose is being annoying. This can be weaponised.

Seagulls will fly around doing random bullshit around the day. And at night turn into a **BLANK** farm.

They will also have an energy  and perhaps a hunger meter. They will both be consumed when doing activities.
If the seagull runs out of energy, it goes back to its nest and falls asleep for a few minutes.

The seagull will have a couple of  daytime behaviours:

- Shitting on players, they will choose a player, fly around them for a while and drop a shit on the player. If said shit hits the player, they will get a render layer and overlay depicting the fact they've been shat on. This can be gotten rid of by entering water for a few seconds and just moving around.

- Screaming, they will choose a player for a longer period of time and switch between walking and flying in the vicinity of the player. Just screaming at them to be obnoxious. 


Seagulls like stealing items from mobs. So by default they will steal items from the [[Shinies tag]]. (**NOTE:** this only applies to items in main hand or offhand).
