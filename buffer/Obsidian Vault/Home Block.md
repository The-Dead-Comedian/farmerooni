
A number of animals like to make homes inside of various blocks. Whether is digging in the ground, creating their own nest or hiding in holes.

Home blocks can store multiple entities that wish to live inside. When a mob enters their home, a copy of the entity is stored in the block. Then the actual entity is discarded. And on exit said copy of the mob is created.

Some home blocks can have gui's and inventories, although not necessary.

Home blocks can only be picked up with silk touch. They will store any mob inside of them. However mobs outside the nest will not be able to go back to their home if it has been relocated.

All homeblocks have a max capacity of mobs it remembers as "members". If the homeblock is not at max capacity of mobs to occupy it, and there is a homeless mob nearby. They will be assigned that block as their home block.
