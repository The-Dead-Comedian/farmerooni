# Sea Gulls

## About
Seagulls are chaotic, cleptomaniac birds that enjoy chaos. While the player can find them useful, they will terrorise everyone, indiscriminately


## Nest
Seagulls spawn on beaches (maybe we add a rock placed feature and some of them have a chance for a nest).
Their nest is a blockentity with an inventory. Sea Gulls set it as their home, while they walk around randomly up to their wish,
but at night they return to their nest to sleep. The player can give the nest a filter item, sea gulls will then steal from the players if they have those kind of item in hand, and return it to their nest.

- If hungry (just a random timer, maybe affected by their activities), they go fishing, looking for a fish that they then dive and steal from the water. If they dont eat (dont have a sea/lake nearby), player gets punished somehow. idk yet
